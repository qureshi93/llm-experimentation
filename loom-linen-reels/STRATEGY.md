# Loom & Linen: 15-Day Pre-Launch Reel Strategy

Site: https://loomlinen.abacusai.app/ . Based on general short-video patterns, not a live trend search.

## What tends to go viral
- **Sensory / ASMR:** fabric crinkle, sheet shaking, slow bed-making.
- **Hot-sleeper relief:** "I sleep hot, so I switched to linen."
- **Before and after:** messy or stiff bed to calm, textured, lived-in bed.
- **Myth-busting / education:** linen softens with washing; linen vs cotton vs microfiber; thread count.
- **Slow living aesthetic:** morning light, rumpled beds, coffee in bed, neutral palette.
- **Behind the scenes:** flax to fabric, quality checks, packing, founder story.
- **Social proof / UGC:** testers, reactions, first-50-orders moments.
- **Relatable humour:** "cotton at 3am vs linen at 3am."

## Strategy
1. **Positioning:** "The cool, calm bed." Linen is the fix for hot, restless sleep. Hook is a problem, payoff is the feeling, product comes second.
2. **Pillars:** Feel (ASMR/aesthetic) ~40%, Fix (sleep, heat, fabric education) ~35%, Brand (story, making, countdown) ~25%.
3. **Format:** hook in first 1.5s with on-screen text; 8-20s; looping ending; captions on every reel; warm neutral natural light; trending audio for Fix/humour, soft ambient or native sound for ASMR.
4. **Follow triggers:** recurring series name; end line "Follow for the launch. Day N of 15."; "Comment LINEN for early access" feeding a waitlist; link-in-bio waitlist with early-bird offer.
5. **Engagement:** reply to every comment (video replies for good questions); Stories polls and countdown sticker; 5-10 micro-creator collaborations with samples; cross-post to TikTok, Shorts, Pinterest; niche tags #linenbedding #hotsleeper #bedroomrefresh #slowliving.
6. **Cadence:** 2/day at ~8am and ~7pm local audience time. AM = Feel or Fix, PM = Brand or Fix. After day 15, double down on best watch time and follows.
7. **Metrics:** watch-through and saves first, then shares, follows per reel, waitlist sign-ups. Review every 3 days; cut the weakest format.

## Production notes
- Batch-film all 30 in 3-4 sessions: one bed set, 3-4 lighting setups.
- Use reel-cli (https://github.com/ParthJadhav/reel-cli) on themed folders for ASMR and montage reels; add text/logo in CapCut or FFmpeg.
- Use `content-calendar.csv` and `caption-template.md`; keep hook, CTA and hashtags consistent.
- Keep a short script bank for Fix reels (under 20s).
- Spend 20-30 min/day on comments and DMs.

## Risks
- No medical claims (e.g. "cures night sweats"). Use "breathable", "feels cooler".
- Only cleared or royalty-free music.
- If a reel flops, test new hooks on the same topic before changing strategy.
